# Model weights, 2026-09-25

Safetensors files on the Ryzen box (AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D, 16 cores, 32
threads) with `scripts/bench_weights.sh`: zstd 1.5.7 (`-T0`) and Glyd
v0.14.9, all cores, wall-clock seconds; every output decoded and
compared, all exact. Raw rows in `benchmarks/weights/rows.tsv`. The
files: EleutherAI's Pythia-410M at training steps 1000, 2000, 70000,
71000, 142000 and 143000 (fp32, 1,621 MB each), Qwen2.5-0.5B and
Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (bf16, 988 MB each).

Glyd opens a safetensors file and holds each tensor as byte planes (a
float's exponent bytes together, its mantissa bytes together). Against
a base (`--base`, and the store), a tensor the base holds under the
same name and size goes in XOR the base's.

| File | zstd -3 | zstd -19 | Glyd `-9` |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pythia-410M step 1000 | 957 MB, 0.8 s | 811 MB, 85 s | **697 MB, 3.3 s** |
| Pythia-410M step 70000 | 959 MB, 0.8 s | 807 MB, 90 s | **701 MB, 3.3 s** |
| Pythia-410M step 143000 | 959 MB, 0.8 s | 809 MB, 89 s | **701 MB, 3.3 s** |
| Qwen2.5-0.5B | 769 MB, 0.3 s | 750 MB, 29 s | **663 MB, 2.5 s** |

| A checkpoint against another | zstd -19 `--patch-from` | Glyd `--max --base` |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Pythia step 2000 against step 1000 | 809 MB, 140 s | **681 MB, 4.9 s** |
| Pythia step 71000 against step 70000 | 805 MB, 148 s | **612 MB, 4.7 s** |
| Pythia step 143000 against step 142000 | 806 MB, 148 s | **501 MB, 4.7 s** |
| Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct against Qwen2.5-0.5B | 748 MB, 57 s | **558 MB, 3.4 s** |

Reads: 1.0–1.5 s a file alone (zstd 1.3–1.6 s), 2.2–3.2 s against a
base (zstd 2.1–2.7 s).

Why: Pythia's fp32 weights hold fp16 values (the low 13 bits of every
weight are zero), and the exponent byte of a trained weight takes few
values. An order-0 coder per tensor and plane needs 682–684 MB for a
Pythia checkpoint and 650 MB for Qwen2.5 (`examples/ai_probe.rs
entropy`); Glyd comes within 2–3% of that, zstd -19 on the file as it
is 18% above. The XOR of consecutive checkpoints clears the bits that
did not move: late in training (a small learning rate) 29% of the
file alone.

Where the bytes are left: the mantissa's high byte is noise (8 bits a
byte in every file); `--ultra --base` on the Qwen2.5 pair comes to 527
MB (29 s), level with XOR then zstd -19.

In the store (`glyd-store DIR --put`, the Ryzen box, local disk): nine
Pythia-410M checkpoints, steps 1000, 2000, 3000, 70000, 71000, 72000,
141000, 142000 and 143000, 14.6 GB, in 55 s: **5.88 GB**, against 7.28
GB for zstd -19 and 8.63 GB for zstd -3 on each file; all nine
verified. Steps 142000 and 143000 went in as deltas of the step before
(501 MB each); the others alone (695–698 MB), their deltas short of the
store's bar (a delta is taken at 80% of the object alone or less: early
and mid training, a step's delta is 88–98%).
