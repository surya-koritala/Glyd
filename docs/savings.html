What Glyd saves you

Bytes stored scale with 1 / ratio. Measured on 2026-09-25 on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D (16 cores, 32 threads) against zstd 1.5.7, LZ4 1.10.0 and gzip 1.14, Glyd at v0.14.9; the bucket row is the store's terabyte gate (v0.14.9, S3, 16 vCPU). Every Glyd output was decoded and compared with its input. Measure your own data with glyd -b yourfile, or a bucket with glyd-store --audit s3://bucket.
