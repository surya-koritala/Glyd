- `qwenserve --gpus 0,1` and Condor's `GLYD_GPUS=0,1 tools/serve.sh` are refused in words: Qwen3.8-Flash-Next runs on one GPU in this release (its layers are not split over several GPUs on the executor yet), and the refusal names the setting and what to give instead (`--gpus 1` / `GLYD_GPUS=1` for the one card to run on). Before, the executor that serves the family ignored the setting and ran on GPU 0; the split over two cards was the family's own engine's, which is gone. `--gpus N` now picks the card; the guide's Condor page says the same.
