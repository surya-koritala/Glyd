- `glyd-engine pack` and `glyd ship` save a model only at a level its family runs, and refuse any other level with the same words `glyd run` gives. GLM-5.3-Flash runs at penguin and hummingbird, so its kestrel, swift, wren and three-bit saves are now refused: nothing loaded them, and their kv_b was in the earlier engine's form. Kimi-K3, GLM-5.3, DeepSeek-V4, Muse Spark and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next were already refused at the lossy levels, but by the packer's list of architectures; they now get the same words as `glyd run`.
