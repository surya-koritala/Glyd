- A conversation's next turn no longer runs from its first token on the models the description executor serves (Gemma 4, Qwen3.8 dense, Muse Spark, Kimi-K3, GLM-5.3-Flash). A finished request's keys, values and states stay in its slot, and a request that begins with its tokens goes on from them: from everything the slot holds where the turn sends the whole reply again; from wherever it leaves the earlier tokens on Gemma 4 and Muse Spark; from the prompt's end on the Qwen3.5 layout (a template that drops the thinking, a reply asked for again with more after the prompt). Kept slots never keep a request waiting: with no slot or no room free the oldest is emptied. `/stats` and `glyd-engine bench`'s `prefix_hit_rate` count the prompt tokens found. `GLYD_ENGINE_KEEP=0` empties a finished request's slot at once, as before.
