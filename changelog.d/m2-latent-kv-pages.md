- Kimi-K3's and GLM-5.3-Flash's latent caches can be held in the lossless 12-bit KV pages (`GLYD_KV=lossless`, as for the dense families): a latent is one stream of the page format, Kimi-K3's 576 values a token in five heads of 128 and GLM-5.3's 512 in four, and the latent attention reads a sequence's pages unpacked to bf16, so its outputs are the bf16 cache's bit for bit. GLM-5.3's indexer keys stay bf16. Not measured on a GPU yet; a latent's decode reads more bytes than with the bf16 cache until the attention reads the 12-bit pages in place.
- The GPU library's `glyd_gpu_kv_unpack` skips a negative page number (C API 21).
