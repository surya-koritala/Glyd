- Several users decoding at once on the description executor: a step's rows that decode take each attention layer in one launch of the preparation and one call of the decode kernel, each row through its own sequence's block table, where there was one of each a sequence a layer. A step of nothing but such rows replays a CUDA graph of its rows padded to 2, 4, 8, ... up to the users served (every size captured before the first request), as one user's step already did, for models made of attention layers and operations on each row alone (Qwen3, Gemma 4, Muse Spark), and of recurrent layers (Qwen3.5, Nemotron-H). On an A100 (40 GB), 8 users' decode tokens/s per user with bf16 pages: gemma-4-12B at wren 48.5 to 83.8, Qwen3.5-0.8B at kestrel 190.4 to 236.1; one user unchanged. Each row's tokens are those of its own pass, bit for bit, over bf16 and 12-bit KV pages alike. `GLYD_PROF=1` prints the attention's decode calls a pass; `GLYD_ENGINE_ROWS_GRAPH=0` (a development build's) runs such steps eager.
