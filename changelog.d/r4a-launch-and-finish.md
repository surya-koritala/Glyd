- A served model's step is launched before the step before it has been read, where every sequence in that step takes its largest logit (greedy, no log-probabilities): the next step's rows read their tokens on the GPU, and the host sends the tokens, ends the sequences that ended and schedules while the GPU runs. A decode step no longer waits on the whole GPU stream: its inputs and samples are copied through pinned memory and the wait is for the step alone, and a block table that changes is copied on the stream. On models with no recurrent state, bf16 pages and no expert cache (Gemma 4, Qwen3 and the other Llama-style dense models, Muse Spark); `GLYD_ENGINE_OVERLAP=0` turns it off. Not measured on a GPU yet.
- `GLYD_PROF=1` and `GLYD_ENGINE_STEP_PROF=1` give each step's GPU time and the GPU's idle time before it (median and mean), from events at the step's first and last work.
- Models split over GPUs (`--split`, `--tp`) and models with a draft model are served by the scheduler too, one request at a time: a drafted model's decode step is a drafting round, and a request that asks for log-probabilities or `min_tokens` is no longer refused on these models (it runs plain steps).
