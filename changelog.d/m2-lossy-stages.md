- The lossy levels (kestrel, swift, wren) of the Qwen3.5 layout, dense or with experts, now cut a model's layers into stages over the GPUs of one machine where one GPU does not hold it, by the same rule penguin's dynamic route cuts with: each layer's bytes at the level with its share of one sequence, each GPU's room its free memory less the reserve and the engine's work, a stage only where one may begin. Every expert stays on the GPUs where they hold them all; otherwise the experts go to the computer's memory and each stage gets its own places of them, by the one expert-cache rule. `fit`, `run` and `serve` print and load the same plan. Stages hold one sequence at a time, as penguin's do. `--split CUTS --devices GPUS` cuts where it says. A cut that is not inside the layers, a GPU that is not there, or a GPU whose stages take more than its room is refused in words. Before, these levels planned one GPU and refused the rest.
- Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B on eight H100s now plans at kestrel, swift and wren: its layers in stages over the eight GPUs, its experts in the computer's memory, places of them on each GPU. Before, each of these levels was refused for needing 88.6 to 121.5 GiB of one GPU. Penguin is still refused: the experts, held lossless, take 3,444.5 GiB and the machine has 1,800 GiB. Qwen3.8-27B at kestrel on two RTX 3090s now plans in two stages, where before one GPU refused it.
- Each stage loads its layers' packs on its own GPU through the Qwen3.5 layout's stage loader, the one penguin's split uses, and the residual is handed over between them. A plan's layout is the one its stages load: a lossy plan's block layout, or penguin's 12-bit or tiered layout.
- `tiercheck --split CUTS --devices GPUS` checks the expert cache in stages, each stage with its own cache, against every expert on one GPU. `splitcheck --dir DIR` checks a model made before. `logitsmodel qwen35moe DIR --real --widths FILE` takes another release's widths (its config.json or its recorded header), such as Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B's, in place of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B's.
