- The GPU library has a prompt attention that reads the lossless 12-bit KV pages where they are (`glyd_gpu_kv_prefill`, C API 19): a chunk's rows over its sequence's cached prefix in the pages and over the chunk's own bf16 rows, causal, for heads of 128, 256 and 512, any number of query heads a kv head, a sliding window, bf16 or fp32 scores, soft-capping and attention sinks. Nothing is unpacked to bf16 first. On an RTX 4080 SUPER, a chunk of 256 rows at gemma-4-12B's shapes after 1,024 / 4,096 / 8,192 tokens: its sliding layers' attention 0.18 ms where unpacking the pages and Gemma 4's kernel take 0.23 (bf16 pages: 0.19); its global layers' 0.47 / 1.65 / 3.26 ms against 0.51 / 1.72 / 3.35 (bf16 pages: 0.49 / 1.70 / 3.30). `kvshapes` holds it to attention on the host from the same values. The engine does not call it yet.
