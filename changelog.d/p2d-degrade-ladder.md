- When a model does not load at the level asked for, the degrade steps are fixed, printed and priced: a shorter context (to the floor), experts in the computer's memory, then a lower level, then a refusal (the KV cache's type and dense layers in memory are not in this engine yet, and `fit` says so). `glyd fit` prints the ladder with each step's cost in speed and in `--json` (`degrade`). `glyd run` says the steps in its first lines and asks before a step that costs over 30% of the speed or lowers the level; `--yes` or `GLYD_DEGRADE=yes` takes them, `GLYD_DEGRADE=no` refuses, and `serve`, the service and a run with no terminal never stop for an answer: without `--yes` they refuse (status 3, as before) naming the steps.
