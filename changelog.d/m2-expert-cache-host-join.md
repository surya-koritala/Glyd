- The expert cache's host path adds a token's experts as copy mode does. Where the host computes some of a call's experts (the `cpu` and `auto` modes; DeepSeek-V4's and Kimi-K3's FP4 experts), each pair's weighted row now goes into the layer's own sum beside the GPU's, and a token's pairs are added in the order of its choices and rounded once, whoever computed them. Before, the GPU's share was summed and rounded to bf16, then the host's share was added and rounded a second time. The error was half a last place of the GPU's partial sum, which, where the pairs cancel, is many last places of the result. Kimi-K3's host pair now rounds where the GPU's does: the second product's fp32 sum times the weight, with no bf16 rounding first, and the activation computed in the GPU's order. Kimi-K3's cached block now traces its experts' choices for the batch checks. Part of #232.
