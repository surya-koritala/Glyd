- Gemma 4 can be served on 12-bit lossless KV pages: `GLYD_KV=lossless` with `--no-draft` (the runs whose cache the scheduler pages). The first run makes the model's KV tables (about 5 s on an RTX 4080 SUPER) and keeps them in Glyd's cache; the plan counts the pages' bytes, so a cache of 3.9 GiB in bf16 is 2.9 GiB. A step after which a page found no room in its pools ends with an error. It is opt-in until admission reserves each request's room and pauses one when the spare pool runs out.
