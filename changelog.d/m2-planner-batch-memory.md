- `serve` plans a Qwen3.5-family or Nemotron-H model for its `--users`: each user's states and context, as the executor gives them. Before, the plan counted one user, and on an A100 SXM4 40GB Qwen3.5-0.8B served to 8 users used 5,054 MiB against a plan of 3,037.
- `glyd-engine plancheck` now reads the GPU memory the server used. On the A100 it printed "memory -" in every case, so its memory gate failed them all.
- Decode for several users at once is predicted as one step for all of them. The step reads the weights once, plus each user's keys and values, plus a per-layer time for each row past the first, fitted from each GPU class's records. Before, the A100's 8-user predictions for Qwen3.5-0.8B and gemma-4-12B were 78-193% too fast. Each class also fits its own figure for the lossless layout: Qwen3-8B on the A100 (its 12-bit layout) had been predicted 28-39% too slow.
- The A100's shipped records are now its runs on the current build: 12 of them, 3 models at 1 and 8 users. Each is predicted within 15% by figures fitted on the other 11 (mean 3.9%).
- `plancheck` takes `--ctx N`. It also predicts what bench measures when several users send their prompts at once: each reply waits for the prompts read after its own, and the first token comes after half of the prompts.
- Every family's reader of config.json (Qwen3, Qwen3.5, Gemma 4, Nemotron-H, GLM-5.3-Flash, Kimi-K3, Muse Spark, DeepSeek-V4 and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next) now reads the one parse that the planner and the refusals use, as do `costs` and the end-token lookups. A shared key of the wrong type is named in the error on every path, with its value.
- A plan where users share pools of blocks (Qwen3, Qwen3.5, Gemma 4, Nemotron-H when served) now holds at most what the GPU has left for their cache. On an A10 (23 GB), Qwen3-8B served to 8 users at 16,384 tokens had been planned at 31,867 MiB; the server took 21,889. 3 of the 2,250 recorded plans change, all three over their GPU before.
- Penguin's decode is now predicted from the layout the loader takes, the 12-bit one everywhere but Ada, read at a share of its own. Before, it was the tiered layout at the RTX A6000's share, and Qwen3-8B on the A10 was predicted 19-25% too slow.
- Prompt speed is now predicted against the GPU's measured memory rate instead of its listed tensor rate. A GPU class with no records takes its prompt figures from every other class.
- With the A10's 20 runs added (36 records in all), each class's runs predicted from the other classes' figures were off by a mean of 18.0% (A100), 19.4% (A10) and 25.1% (RTX 4080 SUPER) for prompts; before, the A10 was 119.7%. Decode was off by a mean of 39.0%, 14.6% and 14.6%.
