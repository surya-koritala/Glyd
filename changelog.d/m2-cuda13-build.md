- The engine builds with CUDA 13 again, as the GPU job and the bundles build it: the Hopper kernels' tensor maps are aligned as the toolkit that built the kernels aligns a `CUtensorMap` (CUDA 13's `cuda.h`: 128 bytes; CUDA 12's: 64), which `build.rs` takes from the version of the nvcc it runs, and every number the engine passes the driver is held to that toolkit's `cuda.h` when the kernels compile. A tensor map is made at an address the driver accepts (64-byte aligned; it was 8). `build.rs` stops where nvcc does not say its version, and compiles the kernels once (no second try with other flags).
- On Windows the engine reads a GPU's TCC mode from the driver's TCC attribute (35; it read 17, the kernels' time limit), so a GPU under WDDM is seen as one: its plans keep 1 GiB more free and `glyd doctor` says so. Part of #134 and #238.
