- CUDA graphs for more of a served model's steps on the description executor. Decode steps of several users on Qwen3.5 and Nemotron-H now replay a graph of their rows padded to 2, 4, 8, ..., as Qwen3, Gemma 4 and Muse Spark already did: the gated delta rule's and Mamba-2's convolution and recurrence take a step's rows in one launch, each row through its own state, with each row's bits those of its own pass. Prompt chunks, steps of several prompts, steps that mix decode rows with a prompt's chunk, and decode steps of models with no whole-step graph run in pieces: the products, norms and experts between two attention or recurrent layers are one graph each, replayed for passes of the same row count, and the attention runs eager between them, so the host queues one launch a piece where it queued every kernel. A replay is the eager pass bit for bit (pieces are captured for a pass's exact rows, not padded). `GLYD_PROF=1` prints the piece graphs replayed a pass; `GLYD_ENGINE_PIECES=0` (a development build's) runs such passes eager.
