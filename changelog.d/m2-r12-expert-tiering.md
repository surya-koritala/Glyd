- DeepSeek-V4, GLM-5.3-Flash and Muse Spark run at penguin with their experts in the computer's memory where their GPUs do not hold them, a cache of the busiest on the GPU (R12): DeepSeek-V4's FP4 experts each in a place of their own, GLM-5.3-Flash's FP8 experts as released each packed on its own (its places at the largest expert's bytes, each place's heads pointed at its own bytes on the GPU after a copy), Muse Spark's lossless experts cut from their stack's one pack (each place its own block entries, multiplied by the GPU library's new `glyd_gpu_mma_moe_placed`; C API version 17), the misses copied in. The expert cache is now a property of the MoE layer of the dynamic route (`MoeLayer::with_cache`). Not yet checked on a GPU; the GPU job compares every logit with the experts resident and in the cache.
- Where even a token's experts do not fit beside a model's other weights on one GPU, `run` keeps the last layers' dense matrices in the computer's memory as well (Muse Spark's attention and latent projections, Kimi-K3's delta attention), copying each into one GPU slot just before it runs. Each such layer costs its bytes over the link every token, and the plan and the run's first lines say so. Not yet checked on a GPU; the GPU job compares logits with two layers held this way.
