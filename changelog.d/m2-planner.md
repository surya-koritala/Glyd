- `fit` predicts decode from the rate this computer's GPU reads its memory at, measured on the GPU's first use (256 MiB read 5 times, the link and the computer's memory likewise; kept in the cache) in place of a table of bandwidths, and shows the prompts' speed and a p10-p90 band. On an RTX 4080 SUPER the predictions were within 3% of what `glyd-engine bench` measured: gemma-4-12B 84.8 tokens/s predicted at wren against 86.2 measured and 62.3 at kestrel against 63.3, Qwen3.5-0.8B 568.7 at kestrel against 561.9 and 675.0 at wren against 657.9, prompts of about 1,000 tokens 3,571 tokens/s against 3,570 (gemma-4-12B) and 26,718 against 27,073 (Qwen3.5-0.8B). The constants are calibrated on these runs. Every reply that runs alone is recorded with the plan that loaded it, and the runs on a GPU then set its predictions and their band.
- Qwen3.5 models were predicted 1.7 times faster than they run: their draft head counted, though no path drafts with it yet. Only Condor's path counts it now.
- `fit --json` has `tokens_per_second_p10`, `tokens_per_second_p90` and `prefill_tokens_per_second`.
- Every path is sized by the planner alone (src/plan.rs): the Qwen3, lossy, Gemma 4 and dynamic-route loaders take its plan, and each of the 765 plans recorded before the change is unchanged. A context asked for that does not fit is refused with the longest that does (`Ask for less: --context N`) on every path; before, only the Qwen3 path said so.
- `glyd run qwen3.5` takes the size `glyd fit` predicts for the machine: 9b on a 16 GB RTX 4080 SUPER, 27b on 24 GB cards and larger.
